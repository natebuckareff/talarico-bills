About this list

This page summarizes 16 enacted bills from Texas’s 2019, 2021, 2023, and 2025 regular legislative sessions on which James Talarico was the lead House author or House sponsor. “House sponsor” means he carried a Senate-originated bill in the House; it does not mean he authored that Senate bill. The record distinguishes sponsorship from votes and co-authorship.

Each bill number links to its official Texas Legislature Online legislative history. The short descriptions are summaries of the legal changes, not independent estimates of how many people benefited, how much money was saved, or any law’s overall merit. For exact eligibility rules, exceptions, enactment details, and implementation status, consult the enrolled text and history.

Unaffiliated with any political campaign or state agency. Prepared as a public reference; last reviewed November 2026.
